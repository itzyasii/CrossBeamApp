CrossBeam sends photos, videos, documents, text, and other files directly between nearby devices.

No account is required. CrossBeam discovers compatible devices on your local network and transfers the content you select without uploading it to CrossBeam cloud storage.

Highlights:
• Send one file or an entire batch
• Receive files on Android phones, tablets, and Android TV
• Share directly to CrossBeam from Gallery, Files, browsers, and other apps
• Pair a phone with Android TV using a QR code
• Approve new senders before accepting files
• Track progress and retry interrupted transfers
• Keep transfer history and settings on your device
• Use the app without ads, accounts, or analytics

Transfers depend on compatible devices and local network conditions. For the current release, use CrossBeam only with devices and networks you trust.
